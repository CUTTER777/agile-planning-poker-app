Workflow
Four steps to consensus
01
Import Backlog
Connect your Jira or Azure DevOps board. Stories load automatically with context and acceptance criteria intact.
02
Open Session
Invite the team via secure link. Participants join without account creation — no friction, no delay.
03
Cast Votes
Everyone votes simultaneously. Cards stay hidden until all participants have submitted, preventing anchoring bias.
04
Record & Sync
Agreed estimates are written back to your issue tracker instantly. Full audit trail retained per sprint.
Capabilities
Built for enterprise delivery
Real-time synchronization
WebSocket-driven sessions with sub-100ms latency. Card reveals, vote counts, and discussion threads update live across all participants.
Jira & ADO integration
Bi-directional sync with Jira Software and Azure DevOps. Estimates are posted directly to story fields without manual entry.
Velocity analytics
Sprint-over-sprint estimation accuracy reports. Identify outliers, track team calibration, and export CSV reports for stakeholder review.
Role-based access
Scrum Masters, Product Owners, and Developers get scoped permissions. Observers can watch without voting. Configurable per workspace.
Full audit trail
Every vote, re-estimate, and discussion thread is logged with timestamps and user identity — satisfying internal compliance and retrospective requirements.
Custom card decks
Fibonacci, T-shirt sizing, or define your own scale. Deck configuration is saved per team and can be locked by workspace administrators.
